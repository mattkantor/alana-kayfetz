How they teach

Every lesson is personalized.

A customized curriculum is designed around each student's academic goals and unique interests.

Direct Instruction

Explicit teaching, taught to mastery.

Teachers break learning into incremental steps and teach each one until it sticks.

Game-based & multi-sensory

Active, engaging, long-lasting.

Most lessons use active and sensory methods that keep students engaged and make learning last.

Executive functioning

Concrete strategies.

Good to know

Questions parents ask before reaching out.

Where is Ruth Rumack's Learning Space?

Ruth Rumack's Learning Space is at 202-40 Holly Street in Toronto's Yonge and Eglinton neighbourhood. Lessons are available in person and online.

What services does Ruth Rumack's Learning Space offer?

It provides personalized instruction and specialized courses in reading, writing, math, keyboarding, handwriting, executive functioning, assistive technology and post-secondary preparation.

What is Direct Instruction?

Direct Instruction is a systematic, research-based teaching approach that breaks skills into manageable steps, teaches them explicitly, and provides structured practice and feedback.

Does Ruth Rumack's Learning Space support students with dyslexia or learning differences?

Yes. The team works with students with a range of learning needs, including ADHD, ASD and all learning disabilities, tailoring instruction to each student's strengths, challenges and academic goals.

Who teaches the lessons?

The team includes Ontario-certified teachers with experience in academic instruction, Direct Instruction and special education. They are full-time teachers rather than part-time tutors.

How long has Ruth Rumack's Learning Space been operating?

Ruth Rumack founded the centre in 1996. Since then it has grown into a specialized academic support centre serving students and families in Toronto and online.

How do I get started?

Visit ruthrumack.com to book a free phone consultation. The team will discuss your child's needs and recommend an appropriate next step.
