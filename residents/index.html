The part nobody counts

There are three businesses on this page. That's the whole roster.

Not a shortlist. Not the featured few. Three. Alana works with a handful of founders at a time because introductions only carry weight when she can vouch for the person on the other end — and you can only vouch for so many people.

What a year outside looks like

  • The podcast guest spot went to someone in her network. You never knew it was open.
  • A brand partnership got introduced to a competitor over coffee.
  • You paid a publicist to pitch strangers who had never heard your name.
  • Your best story stayed in your head, described as "we offer a range of services."
  • Another twelve months of being the best-kept secret in your category.

What a year inside looks like

  • Alana makes the introduction herself, and the other person picks up.
  • Partnership conversations start warm instead of cold.
  • A Mom Halo episode with your name on it, working for years.
  • A page like this one, built to be found by the people already searching.
  • Someone else reads this page next year and wishes they'd moved first.

None of the left-hand column shows up on a P&L. That's exactly why it keeps happening.
