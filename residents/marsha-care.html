Therapy should feel safe enough to tell the truth.— MARSHA Care

There is a season of life that general therapy handles badly: trying to conceive, pregnancy, the postpartum year, birth trauma, loss. MARSHA Care does that season and not much else — which means you never have to start by explaining why it still hurts.

The founder

Built by someone who does this exact work.

Amber Sperling

MSW, RSW, PMH-C, MMHPC · EMDR Certified · Founder

Amber's own clinical focus is birth trauma, reproductive trauma and pregnancy and infant loss — the parts of this season people are least likely to say out loud. She built MARSHA Care so the rest of the team could go equally deep instead of covering everything shallowly.

Most therapy asks how well you're coping. Amber asks what's actually stuck.— MARSHA Care

Who they serve

Two very different people walk through this door.

People in the season

Anywhere in Ontario, usually at the worst possible time to be driving somewhere.

  • Trying to conceive, or carrying the particular grief of infertility
  • Pregnant and more frightened than anyone seems to expect
  • Postpartum, and nothing feels like the version you were promised
  • A birth that went wrong and still replays without warning
  • Pregnancy or infant loss that the people around you have stopped mentioning
  • A couple who became parents and can't find each other any more

Therapists

The audience most private practices don't have at all.

  • Clinicians building their own perinatal mental health practice
  • Therapists who want supervision from someone specialised, not generalist
  • Practitioners going deeper on EMDR and reproductive trauma

Running clinical supervision alongside client work is what turns a practice into a small institution — and it's a second revenue line that doesn't depend on Amber's own calendar.

Good to know

Questions people ask before reaching out.

Where does MARSHA Care offer therapy?

MARSHA Care offers virtual therapy to clients anywhere in Ontario, plus in-person appointments in Barrie and Orillia.

What does MARSHA Care specialise in?

Perinatal and reproductive mental health — pregnancy and postpartum support, birth trauma, pregnancy and infant loss, infertility counselling, EMDR and trauma therapy, and couples therapy.

Who founded MARSHA Care?

Amber Sperling, MSW, RSW, PMH-C, MMHPC, an EMDR-certified social worker specialising in birth trauma, reproductive trauma and perinatal mental health.

What is EMDR therapy?

EMDR, or Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing, is a structured trauma therapy that helps the brain reprocess distressing memories so they stop intruding on the present. Several MARSHA Care clinicians are trained in it, and it is used frequently for birth trauma and reproductive trauma.

Does MARSHA Care offer clinical supervision for therapists?

Yes. Alongside client care, MARSHA Care provides clinical supervision for therapists building their own perinatal mental health practice.

How do I book with MARSHA Care?

Book a free consultation through marshacare.ca, which matches you with the clinician on the team whose specialties fit what you are carrying.
