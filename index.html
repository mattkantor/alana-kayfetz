About Alana

Hey, I'm Alana!

I'm a coach, a connector and the host of Mom Halo. I started in events and marketing, and I've spent the last decade building a community of women who are raising kids and building businesses at the same time.

I'm also the co-founder of The Golda Club, a community for Jewish women with big lives, bold visions and full hearts, and home of the Golda Club Summit in Toronto.

That community is the point. Founders work with me to grow through the Kayfetz Method and to get known through my network. Brands like Apple, Joe Fresh and Elizabeth Grant work with me because those same women trust what I recommend.

My mission hasn't changed: enrich women's lives with exceptional experiences that put well-being and joy first.
