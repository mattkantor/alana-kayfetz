Midtown Toronto · In clinic & virtual

Everything your body and mind need, on one floor.

Most people end up with a therapist downtown, a massage therapist near work, and a chiropractor they found in a panic. Breathe put all eleven under one roof at 1905 Avenue Road and had them actually talk to each other.

The founder

Elise Devlin

MSW, RSW, MHSc · Founder & Therapist

Elise is a registered social worker, and Breathe began as her virtual therapy and counselling practice. The clinic on Avenue Road came afterwards — built because the people she was already treating kept needing the other half of the care too.

That order matters. The bodywork, the osteopathy and the skin therapy were added around a clinical practice, not bolted together into a spa.

What they do

Three kinds of care that usually live in three different buildings.

Mind

Therapy and counselling with registered social workers — the practice's original service, still delivered virtually as well as in clinic.

Body

Registered massage therapy, lymphatic drainage, athletic therapy, fascial stretch therapy, osteopathy and chiropractic. Relief on its own, or a coordinated plan.

Restore

Customized facials and skin therapy, lash and brow, reiki and restorative yoga — with clean, hand-picked skincare rather than whatever the distributor is pushing.

Who it's for

Midtown Toronto, and anyone in Ontario who'd rather not explain their history four separate times.

  • You want mental and physical health handled by people who share notes, not by four unconnected clinics
  • You're coming in for one specific thing — a sore back, a hard month — and want it dealt with properly
  • You want evenings and weekends, because a weekday 2pm appointment is not a real option
  • You'd rather have a coordinated plan than a loyalty card

A highly tailored, seamless experience akin to a health and wellness concierge.— Breathe Therapy and Wellness

Breathe started as a virtual therapy and counselling practice. The clinic came later, once it was obvious that clients wanting help with their heads also wanted help with their shoulders, their skin and their sleep — and were tired of chasing it across the city.

Good to know

Questions people ask before booking.

Where is Breathe Therapy and Wellness located?

Breathe Therapy and Wellness is at 1905 Avenue Road, 2nd Floor, in midtown Toronto, Ontario M5M 3Z9.

What services does Breathe Therapy and Wellness offer?

Eleven disciplines: therapy and counselling, registered massage therapy, lymphatic drainage massage, athletic therapy, fascial stretch therapy, osteopathy, chiropractic, customized facials and skin therapy, lash and brow services, reiki, and restorative yoga.

Does Breathe Therapy and Wellness offer virtual appointments?

Yes. The practice began as a virtual therapy and counselling service and still offers virtual care Monday to Friday, 9am to 10pm, alongside in-person appointments at the Toronto clinic.

Who founded Breathe Therapy and Wellness?

Elise Devlin, MSW, RSW, MHSc, a registered social worker who still practises as a therapist at the clinic. Breathe began as her virtual therapy and counselling practice before expanding into the midtown Toronto clinic.

What are the clinic hours?

The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 10am to 8pm, and Saturday and Sunday from 10am to 9pm. Virtual care runs Monday to Friday, 9am to 10pm.

How do I book an appointment at Breathe Therapy and Wellness?

Book online at breathetherapy.janeapp.com, or call or text 647-576-7557. You can also email info@breathetherapy.ca.
